What MeasureOne is

MeasureOne is a practice planner for musicians learning new pieces. It turns what you enter into a day-by-day plan and changes the plan as you log practice. The plans and confidence figures are suggestions based on what you tell it. They are not a promise of results and not a teacher's advice.
