Technique practice

Interactive mockup of the scales-and-arpeggios feature. Use the sidebar to move between the Master Agenda, Technique, Daily Practice, and Settings; the piece switcher swaps the open piece. Everything here is sample data and resets when the page reloads.

The sample day

Today's list follows the priority order: a repertoire-key scale first (G minor, from the Ballade), a slow scale next (E♭ major arpeggio), and the circle-of-fifths walk last (D major, then B minor). The real cards carry no tier labels.

Sample data

Sixteen library items, thirteen practice methods, and two pieces. Star a method or a scale, check tasks off on any screen, or change a scale's check octaves in the Library to see the keep-or-start-fresh prompt.

Not shown

The Add scale or arpeggio form, the setup wizard's key field, and settings for scales per day and minutes per scale. Buttons with no behavior described here do nothing.
